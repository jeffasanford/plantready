Privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026

The short version

  • Your answers and resume are saved on your own device, not on our servers.
  • What you type is sent to our AI provider only to write your resume or feedback.
  • Stripe handles payments. We never see your card number.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads or use tracking cookies.

Who we are

PlantReady is a service of Aspis LLC, a Louisiana company. In this policy, "we" means Aspis LLC.

What you type

When you answer interview questions, paste a job posting or practice an answer, that text is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, so it can write your resume, tailor it or score your answer. The result comes back to your browser. Anthropic handles these requests under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models by default. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Please don't type things a resume doesn't need, like your Social Security number, birth date or medical information.

What's saved on your device

So you can leave and come back, your browser keeps your interview answers, resume draft, chosen region, work type and plan access. This stays in your browser's storage on that device. We don't receive a copy.

Tap Start fresh to erase it, especially on a shared or work computer. Clearing your browser data or using private browsing also erases it. We can't recover a lost draft, so download your resume once it's built.

Subscriptions and payments

Plans are monthly subscriptions processed by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details and the email you enter at checkout, and keeps a record of your subscription. We can see your email, plan and payment status, but never your card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.

You can cancel anytime with Manage or cancel. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

If you clear your browser or switch devices, use Restore purchase with your checkout email to unlock an active subscription again. Anyone who enters that email can restore access, so use an email address you control.

Job listings

Live job openings come from Adzuna. We send them only your chosen region and work type, not anything about you. When you open a listing, you leave PlantReady, and the job site's own privacy policy applies.

Worker stories

If you share your story, it goes to us by email from your own email app. We post it only if you check the permission box, and only your first name, trade, area and story. Never your email or last name. To have a story taken down, email us and we'll remove it.

Emails to us

If you email us, we keep the message and your address so we can help you. We won't add you to a mailing list or share your address.

Server logs

Our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps standard logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These are used for security and to fix problems, and are kept for a short time.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't run ads, use advertising or tracking cookies, or share your information with employers or recruiters.

Your choices

You can erase what's on your device at any time with Start fresh. To ask what we hold about you, or to delete your Stripe customer record or a posted story, email us. We'll answer within 30 days. Records Stripe must keep for tax or legal reasons may be kept as long as the law requires.

Who PlantReady is for

PlantReady is meant for adults looking for work. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

If the business changes hands

If PlantReady is sold or merged, information we hold at that time may transfer to the new owner, who must follow this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
