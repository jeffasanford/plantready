What you type

When you answer interview questions, paste a job posting, or practice an answer, that text is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) so it can write your resume or feedback. The result comes back to your browser.

What we keep

We don't save your answers or your resume on our servers. Your region choice is remembered in your own browser so you don't have to pick it again. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (like IP addresses) for security for a short time.

Your purchase and access

Payments are handled by Stripe. We don't see or store your card number. Stripe keeps a record of your purchase, including the email you enter at checkout.

Your interview answers, resume draft and access are saved only in the browser on your device, so you can leave and come back. Tap Start fresh to erase them, especially on a shared computer.

Your access and your resume are saved only in the browser on your device. If you clear your browser data, use private browsing, or switch devices, use Restore purchase with your checkout email to unlock an active purchase again. Anyone who enters that email can restore access to the purchase, so use an email address you control. We can't recover resume drafts, so download your resume once it's built.

What our AI provider keeps

Anthropic processes requests under its commercial terms and does not use API data to train its models by default. See Anthropic's privacy policy for details.

Selling the business

If PlantReady is sold or merged, any information we hold at that time may transfer to the new owner, who must follow this policy.
